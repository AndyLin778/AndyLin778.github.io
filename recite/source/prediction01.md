# 一、这类图画的思路，是怎么一步步出来的

拿到一张"对比型图画"（两个人、两种做法、两种结果），**按这个顺序想，不要一上来就想英文**：

| 步 | 动作 | 这道题的具体结果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **找结构**：图里是几个人？几组？ | 2 个人，**1 组对比**（磨刀的 vs 硬砍的） |
| **2** | **找结果差**：两人的结果分别是什么？ | 左边**一堆柴**，右边**只有几根** |
| **3** | **找文字**：图下有字吗？写的什么？ | **磨刀不误砍柴工** |
| **4** | **把文字翻成"道理"**（不是翻成英文，是先翻成人话） | 花时间准备 ≠ 浪费时间；准备反而让人更快 |
| **5** | **定一句话中心**（这句决定第二、三段写什么） | **"磨刀不误砍柴工：准备不浪费时间，反而提高效率。"** |
| **6** | **给三段分配词数** | 描述 45–55 ／ 解读 75–90 ／ 评论 40–50 |
| **7** | **每段选句架，填空** | 见第二、三、四节 |

### 第 4 步最容易做错

初学者往往直接从"磨刀不误砍柴工"跳到"我们要努力学习"，**中间那一步"为什么磨刀更快"没想清楚**，第二段就写空了。

**正确的中间步骤**：

> 磨刀 → 刀更快 → 同样的力气砍更多柴
> 所以：**准备不是"用掉"时间，而是提高后面每一分钟的效率**

**把这句话想清楚了，第二段自然有话说**，而且不会写成空喊口号。

### 这道题属于哪一类话题？

找到话题归属，才知道该调哪组词。这道题属于：

| 话题类 | 关键词 | 对应词块组 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **方法 / 效率 / 准备** | efficiency, method, preparation, strategy | 第六节 D |
| 品格 / 坚持（次要） | perseverance, patience | 可用，但不是主线 |

> ⚠️ **不要往"环保""文化交流""科技"上靠**。这张图跟环保没关系（Repair Café 那篇才是环保角度）。

# 二、第一段：描述（45–55 词，两件事：对比 + 文字）

## 描述公式（对比型图画专用）

```
框架句（这是对比） → 一方在做什么（细节） → 另一方在做什么（细节，反差词）
→ 双方结果差 → 图下文字
```

**四要素，缺一个就少一分：**

| 要素 | 这道题怎么填 |
| --- | --- |
| ① **结构**：这是对比 | `presents a striking contrast between two woodcutters` |
| ② **一方** + 细节 | `sitting on a tree stump, sharpening his axe on a whetstone` + `a pile of split firewood at his feet` |
| ③ **另一方** + 细节 | `straining to cut a log with a blunt, chipped blade` + `sweat streams down his face` + `only a few pieces of wood` |
| ④ **图下文字** | `The caption reads, "Sharpening the axe does not delay the woodcutting."` |

## 三种描述写法（都合格，选一种练熟）

### 写法 A · 标准版（最稳，推荐先练这个）

> The drawing presents a striking contrast between two woodcutters. Sitting on a tree stump, one man is calmly sharpening his axe on a whetstone, with a neat pile of split firewood at his feet. On the right, another is straining to cut a log with a blunt, chipped blade. Sweat streams down his face, yet only a few pieces of wood lie beside him. The caption reads, "Sharpening the axe does not delay the woodcutting."

**好在哪**：`Sitting on a tree stump` 用分词开头，句式有变化；`yet` 一句带出反差；结果和文字都齐。

### 写法 B · 对比前置版（句式更漂亮，细节偏少）

> As is vividly depicted in the picture, two woodcutters are working side by side. The one on the left has paused to sharpen his axe before going on with his work, while the one on the right keeps striking a log with a dull blade, sweating heavily yet making little progress. The caption reads, "Sharpening the axe does not delay the woodcutting."

**好在哪**：`The one on the left ... while the one on the right ...` 一句就把对比钉死。
**代价**：细节比写法 A 少，字数不够时要在第二段补。

### 写法 C · 细节版（画面感强，适合想要 16 分以上）

> The drawing shows two woodcutters in ancient dress. One sits on a tree stump, calmly rubbing his axe against a whetstone; sparks fly from the blade, and a large stack of split firewood has already been piled beside him. Not far away, the other man swings a blunt and chipped axe at a log again and again, sweat pouring down his face, but the wood under his blade barely splits. The caption reads, "Sharpening the axe does not delay the woodcutting."

**好在哪**：`sparks fly`、`barely splits` 这类细节让描述"活"起来。
**代价**：词数偏多（约 70 词），会挤占第二段，慎用。

## 描述段的三个常见错误

| 错 | 例子 | 改法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 只写一个人 | 整段都在写磨刀的人 | 对比型图画**必须两个人**，否则"对比"不成立 |
| 漏图下文字 | 没写 `The caption reads` | 这是**硬性内容分**，必写 |
| 把"磨刀"写错 | `repairing his axe` / `washing his axe` | 磨刀是 **`sharpening his axe`**，磨刀石是 **`whetstone`** |

# 三、第二段：解读（75–90 词，这是拉开差距的一段）

## 解读的三层结构

```
① 转折点出"表面 vs 实际"   （第一层）
② 说明为什么磨刀更快         （第二层，讲机制）
③ 从两人扩到所有人          （第三层，社会现象）
```

**为什么必须要这三层**：第一层解决"看图谁都会说的话"，第二层证明你**真懂这个道理**，第三层证明你能**推广**。第三层是 15 分和 12 分的分界。

## 四组可选观点（选 1–2 组，不要全塞）

### 观点一：准备不是浪费时间（最贴题）

> At first glance, the man on the left seems to be wasting time, since no wood is being cut. In fact, sharpening the axe is exactly what enables him to work efficiently: a sharp blade cuts deeper with the same effort. The other man, in contrast, mistakes sweaty labour for hard work, and his dull tool turns every heavy blow into wasted energy.

中文：乍看之下，左边的人似乎浪费时间，因为没在砍柴。事实上，磨斧头正是让他高效率工作的原因：刀快了，同样的力气能砍得更深。相反，另一个人把满头大汗当成努力，而钝了的工具让每一次重砍都变成白费力气。

### 观点二：方法比蛮力重要

> What the picture reveals is that method matters more than mere effort. Two men spend the same afternoon in the woods, yet one finishes a whole pile of firewood while the other leaves with a few sticks. The difference does not lie in their strength, but in whether they are willing to pause and prepare.

中文：这幅画揭示的是方法比单纯的力气更重要。两个人在林子里花掉同样的一个下午，一个砍完一整堆柴，另一个只带出几根。差别不在力气，而在于他们是否愿意停下来做准备。

### 观点三：效率社会的反讽（想上 16 分用这个）

> In a society that praises visible busyness, people easily confuse fatigue with effort and speed with efficiency. The sweating woodcutter looks more hard-working, yet he is trapped in repetitive labour with diminishing returns. Real progress often comes from the unglamorous work no one watches — thinking, planning and improving one's tools.

中文：在一个崇尚"看得见的忙碌"的社会里，人们很容易把疲惫当成努力、把速度当成效率。满头大汗的樵夫看起来更勤奋，但他困在回报递减的重复劳动里。真正的进步往往来自没人看见的那些不体面的工作——思考、计划和改进工具。

### 观点四：迁移到自己（考试落点，最真诚）

> This is not merely a story about woodcutters. Students who memorise thousands of words without reviewing their methods may work far harder than those who take time to build a system, and end up with far less to show.

中文：这不仅是樵夫的故事。背了上千单词却从不反思方法的学生，可能比那些花时间建立体系的人努力得多，结果却少得多。

## 让步句（三种写法，选一种）

一段里放一句让步，立刻从 13 分档往上走：

| 写法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| `Admittedly..., nevertheless...` | `Admittedly, sharpening takes time and yields nothing visible; nevertheless, it is the very thing that makes the later work pay off.` |
| `It is true that..., but...` | `It is true that the man on the left has stopped cutting; but stopping is not the same as wasting time.` |
| `Some may argue..., yet...` | `Some may argue that action matters more than preparation; yet action without method only multiplies wasted effort.` |

# 四、第三段：评论（40–50 词，三件事）

```
表态（In my view） → 落到一个具体场景 → 收尾（倒装句最有劲）
```

## 三个不同落点（选一个，不要都写）

### 落点 A · 考试备考（对你最有用）

> In my view, this lesson matters most to those of us preparing for examinations. Many candidates rush at tasks with a blunt mind, unwilling to spend an hour improving their methods. Only by sharpening our skills and planning our strategy can we cut far more wood in far less time.

### 落点 B · 职场与技能

> In my view, the picture speaks directly to the modern workplace. Those who keep upgrading their skills adapt quickly, while those who rely on outdated tools exhaust themselves. Only by sharpening the axe from time to time can we stay efficient in a changing world.

### 落点 C · 通用人生（最安全）

> In my view, the real gap between the two men lies not in diligence but in wisdom. Rest and reflection are not indulgences; they are part of the work. Only when we are willing to stop and sharpen our axes can we make genuine progress.

# 五、完整范文（198 词，打磨版）

> 这篇刻意避开 `First… Second… In my view… Only in this way…` 的流水线：**每段都带一个自己的判断**，第三段直接说"我们其实在夸错人"，而不是喊口号。
> 三段分别 **73 / 63 / 62** 词。

> Two woodcutters work in one clearing. On the left, a man sits on a stump, running his axe across a whetstone; at his feet a stack of split firewood has already risen. On the right, another drives a blunt, chipped blade into the same crack again and again. Sweat runs down his jaw, yet the pile beside him is barely a handful. The caption reads: "Sharpening the axe does not delay the woodcutting."
>
> At first glance the left-hand man is the idle one. He is not cutting anything, only rubbing metal against stone. But a keen edge is made exactly that way: attention applied before the work begins. The same swing that glances off a dull blade splits the log when the blade is sharp. Two men, one afternoon, between them only ten minutes of preparation.
>
> Yet we tend to praise the sweating man, because effort is visible and preparation is not. Preparing looks like standing still. Students fall into the same trap: an hour spent reorganising how we study may save ten hours of grinding, yet it feels like waste. The willingness to stop and sharpen is itself a skill, and a far rarer one than endurance.

## 每段在干什么

| 段 | 词数 | 任务 | 这一段的"真东西"（不是套话） |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 第 1 段 | 73 | 描述 | 用**两个具体结果**完成对比：一堆柴 vs 一只手能捧住的几根 |
| 第 2 段 | 63 | 解读 | **先承认"他看起来在偷懒"**，再指出锋刃正是"工作前的注意力"做出来的 |
| 第 3 段 | 62 | 评论 | 不说"要努力"，而说**我们夸的是看得见的东西**，缺陷在我们自己的评判标准里 |

> 第 2 段开头那句 `At first glance ... is the idle one` 是全文最关键的设计：**先把读者心里的话说出来，再推翻它**。这比 `This picture tells us that preparation is important` 强出一个档。

## 逐句对照：每句在干什么 + 能复用成什么

| 句序 | 这句在干什么 | 可复用句架 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-1 | 一句定场（时间/地点/人物） | `Two ___ work in one ___.` |
| 1-2 | 左边：动作 + 已得结果 | `On the left, a man sits on ___, ___ing; at his feet ___ has already risen.` |
| 1-3 | 右边：只有动作，暗示徒劳 | `On the right, another drives a ___ into the same ___ again and again.` |
| 1-4 | **用 `yet` 一句带出结果反差** | `___, yet the pile beside him is barely a handful.` |
| 1-5 | **图下文字**（硬性要点） | `The caption reads: "..."` |
| 2-1 | **先替读者说出"看似"的判断** | `At first glance ___ is the ___ one.` |
| 2-2 | 把这个判断说足（不给对方反驳空间） | `He is not ___ing anything, only ___ing.` |
| 2-3 | **转折，给机制** | `But ___ is made exactly that way: ___ applied before ___.` |
| 2-4 | 用同一个动作对比两种结果 | `The same ___ that ___ off a ___ ___ when ___.` |
| 2-5 | 收束，把差距量化成"十分钟" | `Two ___, one ___, between them only ___ of ___.` |
| 3-1 | 指出我们的认知偏差 | `Yet we tend to praise ___, because ___ is visible and ___ is not.` |
| 3-2 | 补一句让偏差更刺眼 | `___ looks like ___.` |
| 3-3 | 迁移到"我们"（考试落点） | `___ fall into the same trap: an hour spent ___ may save ten hours of ___, yet it feels like ___.` |
| 3-4 | **定性收尾，不喊口号** | `The willingness to ___ is itself a ___, and a far rarer one than ___.` |

# 六、素材库（按功能分类，尽量多）

> 这一节只放**可以直接抄走、改一改就能用**的东西，不放建议。每条都给中文，方便你判断该在什么时候取用。

## 6.1 图画描述句（12 句）

**1.** `As is vividly depicted in the picture, ...`
如图画生动描绘的那样，……

**2.** `The drawing depicts two men at work in a clearing.`
这幅画描绘了两个男人在林间空地干活。

**3.** `In the picture, one man ..., while the other ...`
画中一人……，而另一人……

**4.** `Running his axe across a whetstone, the man on the left ...`
一名男子把斧头在磨刀石上来回磨着，他……
> 分词开头，句式立刻不平。

**5.** `Sweat running down his face, he keeps swinging.`
汗水顺着脸颊流下，他仍不停挥砍。
> **独立主格**（`名词 + doing`），高级语法点，写对就是加分。

**6.** `His brows are locked in effort.`
他眉头紧锁，一脸吃力。

**7.** `The pile beside him is barely a handful.`
他身边的柴堆只有寥寥几根。

**8.** `The contrast could hardly be sharper.`
这种对比再鲜明不过。

**9.** `The caption reads: "..."`
图下文字写着："……"（**图画题必写**）

**10.** `The words beneath the picture say, "..."`
图下方的文字说："……"

**11.** `Simple as the picture is, the meaning behind it is far from simple.`
尽管图画简单，其背后的含义绝不简单。

**12.** `At his feet a stack of split firewood has already risen.`
他脚边已经堆起一摞劈好的柴。

### 图表描述句（12 句，备着——近三年真题是图表题）

**13.** `The chart shows the changes in ... between ... and ...`
该图展示了……在……到……之间的变化。

**14.** `What stands out most is that ...`
最突出的一点是……

**15.** `A ranks first at 46.3%, followed by B at 24.9%.`
A 以 46.3% 位居首位，其次是占 24.9% 的 B。

**16.** `The remaining 27.9% ...`
其余 27.9% 的人……

**17.** `Compared with 2020, the figure nearly doubled.`
与 2020 年相比，这一数字几乎翻倍。

**18.** `The figures speak for themselves.`
数字本身说明了一切。

**19.** `Nearly one in three respondents ...`
近三分之一的受访者……

**20.** `The two charts point to the same conclusion: ...`
两张图指向同一个结论：……

**21.** `It is worth noting that ...`
值得注意的是……

**22.** `This trend, if left unchecked, may ...`
这一趋势若不加约束，可能……

**23.** `Only 10.7% of them regard convenience as their main concern.`
只有 10.7% 的人把便利视为主要关注点。

**24.** `The proportion of those who ... declined sharply, from 45% to 20%.`
……的比例急剧下降，从 45% 降至 20%。

## 6.2 对比与反差句型（14 句）

**25.** `The one on the left ..., while the one on the right ...`
左边那位……，而右边那位……

**26.** `In contrast, ...`
相反，……

**27.** `What separates them is not strength but method.`
把他们区分开的不是力气，而是方法。

**28.** `The difference lies not in effort but in the tool.`
差别不在于努力，而在于工具。

**29.** `One works hard; the other works well.`
一个很用力，一个很会用。

**30.** `It is not that he is lazy; it is that he stopped to prepare.`
不是他懒，而是他停下来做准备了。

**31.** `The harder he swings, the less he gains.`
他砍得越猛，收获越少。
> `the + 比较级, the + 比较级`，好写又好用。

**32.** `Two people, the same afternoon, very different results.`
两个人，同一个下午，结果截然不同。

**33.** `Side by side, the two men tell two different stories.`
并排看，这两个人讲的是两个不同的故事。

**34.** `The gap between them is ten minutes, not ten hours.`
他们之间的差距是十分钟，不是十小时。

**35.** `... , whereas ...`
……，而……（`whereas` 比 `but` 书面）

**36.** `For all his effort, he makes little progress.`
尽管很努力，他却进展甚微。
> `for all` = 尽管，很容易用出来。

**37.** `He finishes first not because he works faster, but because he wastes less.`
他先完成，不是因为干得快，而是因为浪费得少。

## 6.3 解释机制（说清"为什么"，12 句）

> 这一段是**解读段的核心**。只会说"我们应该努力"的人，就是卡在这里。

**38.** `This is exactly what a keen edge is made of — attention applied before the work begins.`
锋刃正是这样形成的——在工作开始之前先投入注意力。

**39.** `The same swing glances off a dull blade and splits the log when the blade is sharp.`
同样的挥砍，钝刃只是滑开，刃快就能劈开木头。

**40.** `Preparation does not consume time; it multiplies it.`
准备不消耗时间，它让时间倍增。

**41.** `Ten minutes now buy back an hour later.`
现在的十分钟，能换回后来的一小时。

**42.** `Without it, every blow lands on the wrong thing.`
没有它，每一次用力都落在错的地方。

**43.** `He is not resting; he is investing.`
他不是在休息，他是在投资。

**44.** `A sharp tool turns effort into results; a blunt one turns effort into noise.`
利器把力气变成结果，钝器把力气变成噪音。

**45.** `The problem is not how hard he works, but what he works with.`
问题不在于他多努力，而在于他用什么在干。

**46.** `Efficiency is the ratio of result to effort, not the amount of sweat.`
效率是结果与投入的比值，不是汗水的多少。

**47.** `Most wasted effort is not laziness; it is method applied too late.`
大多数白费的力气不是懒惰，而是方法来得太晚。

**48.** `A dull blade forces the body to make up for what the mind skipped.`
钝刃逼着身体去弥补头脑跳过的步骤。

**49.** `This explains why the busier man finishes last.`
这就解释了为什么更忙的那个人最后完成。

## 6.4 让步与反驳（9 句）

**50.** `Admittedly, ...; nevertheless, ...`
诚然，……；然而，……

**51.** `It is true that ...; but it does not follow that ...`
确实……；但这并不意味着……

**52.** `Granted that ... , ...`
就算……，……

**53.** `Some will object that ...; yet ...`
有人会反对说……；然而……

**54.** `The objection has force, but only up to a point.`
这个反对有道理，但只在某种程度上。

**55.** `One might argue that action matters more than preparation; the picture suggests otherwise.`
有人可能说行动比准备更重要；这幅画提示的相反。

**56.** `It would be unfair to call him lazy — and equally unfair to call him effective.`
说他懒不公平，说他高效也同样不公平。

**57.** `Of course ... , but that is not the whole story.`
当然……，但这不是全部。

**58.** `Hard as he tries, the wood barely splits.`
尽管他很努力，木头几乎劈不开。
> **让步倒装**：`形容词 + as + 主语 + 动词`。

## 6.5 评论与收尾（14 句，全部避开模板腔）

> 把你原来那句 `In my view, ... Only in this way can we ...` 换掉，从这里挑。

**59.** `Yet we tend to praise the sweating man, because effort is visible and preparation is not.`
然而我们往往夸赞那个满头大汗的人，因为努力看得见，而准备看不见。

**60.** `The willingness to stop and sharpen is itself a skill, and a far rarer one than endurance.`
愿意停下来磨刀本身就是一种能力，而且比忍耐稀缺得多。

**61.** `The irony is that we reward the visible.`
讽刺的是，我们奖励看得见的东西。

**62.** `Busyness photographs well; preparation does not.`
忙碌上镜，准备不上镜。

**63.** `Results follow the blade, not the sweat.`
结果跟的是刀刃，不是汗水。

**64.** `What looks like idleness is often the most productive hour of the day.`
看起来像闲着的那个小时，常常是一天里产出最高的。

**65.** `We praise the wrong things, and then wonder why effort does not turn into progress.`
我们夸错了东西，然后又奇怪为什么努力没有变成进步。

**66.** `A quiet hour of planning outlasts a noisy week of trying.`
安静地计划一小时，比热闹地尝试一周更管用。

**67.** `To work hard is a virtue; to work well is a rarer one.`
努力是一种美德，会用力是一种更稀缺的美德。

**68.** `The man with the whetstone is doing the unglamorous arithmetic that the other refuses.`
磨刀的那个人在算一笔不体面的账，另一个不肯算。

**69.** `In exam preparation, an hour spent reorganising how we study can save ten hours of grinding.`
在备考中，花一小时重整学习方法，可能省下十小时的苦熬。

**70.** `He finishes first not by moving faster, but by wasting less.`
他先完成，不是靠更快，而是靠少浪费。

**71.** `The lesson is not "work harder" but "notice what your axe is doing".`
这道理的落脚点不是"更努力"，而是"看看你的斧头在干什么"。

**72.** `Endurance without method is just a longer way to the same place.`
没有方法的忍耐，只是通往同一个地方更长的路。

## 6.11 三个"不像模板"的收尾（同一篇可用任一个）

**版本 A（现在范文用的，最自然）**
> Yet we tend to praise the sweating man, because effort is visible and preparation is not. Preparing looks like standing still. Students fall into the same trap: an hour spent reorganising how we study may save ten hours of grinding, yet it feels like wasted time. The willingness to stop and sharpen is itself a skill, and a far rarer one than endurance.

**版本 B（更克制，学术腔）**
> The habit of praising effort over method is old, and expensive. In preparation for examinations, an hour spent reorganising how we study often returns more than ten hours of grinding; but it looks like idleness, and few of us count it as work. The man with the whetstone is doing that unglamorous arithmetic. The one beside him pays for refusing to.

**版本 C（短促，有力度）**
> The irony is that we reward the visible. Busyness photographs well; preparation does not. But results follow the blade, not the sweat — and the man who stops to sharpen is not behind. He is simply working on the part of the job that no one applauds.

**版本 D（落到"考试"，最贴你自己）**
> This is not a lesson about woodcutters. Candidates who reread the same notes ten times may outwork those who take an afternoon to redesign how they study, and still finish behind. Preparation feels like a delay precisely because its payoff is invisible at the moment it is made. That is also why it is worth learning.

# 八、Part A 的思路（第 51 题，10 分，约 100 词）

## 题面原文

> **51. Directions:**
> Suppose your university is going to hold a "Repair Café" on campus, where volunteers help people fix broken things for free. Write an email to the international students on campus to (1) introduce the event, and (2) invite them to take part as volunteers.
>
> You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead. Do not write your address. (10 points)

## 思路五步

| 步 | 想什么 | 这题的答案 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 审题：谁写给谁** | 收件人是一群人还是一个人？ | **国际学生（一群人）** → 称呼 `Dear international students,` |
| **2 审题：几个任务** | 题目里有几个动词？ | **2 个**：`introduce` + `invite`。**这两个必须各占一部分** |
| **3 活动是什么** | 用自己的话讲清楚 Repair Café | 志愿者**免费**帮人**修东西**，修好就不用扔 |
| **4 邀请什么** | 邀请他们"做什么" | 当**志愿者**（不是当参加者）——这一点极易写错 |
| **5 落款** | 认识对方吗？ | 不认识 → **`Yours faithfully,`** + `Li Ming` |

## 两个致命错

| 错 | 后果 |
| --- | --- |
| **只写了"介绍"，没写"邀请当志愿者"** | 第 2 个要点缺失，内容分直接掉档 |
| **把"邀请他们参加"写成"邀请他们来参观"** | 题目要的是 `take part as volunteers`（**当志愿者**），不是来当客人 |

> 记住：`take part **as volunteers**` —— as volunteers 这四个字母决定你有没有答对题。

## Repair Café 的中文思路

- 字面：修理咖啡馆
- 实质：**志愿者免费帮人修东西**的活动，修好就不用买新的 → 省钱 + 环保 + 邻里互助
- 你可以写的三个角度（选 1–2 个）：
  1. **实用**：修好还能用，省钱
  2. **环保**：`repair rather than replace`，减少浪费（reduce waste）
  3. **互助**：`a chance to meet people and share skills`

# 九、Part A 三种写法 + 完整范文（95 词）

## 第一部分（介绍活动）的三种写法

**写法 1（定义式，最稳）**
> I am writing to introduce a special event called "Repair Café", which will be held in the Students' Centre next Saturday afternoon.

**写法 2（目的式）**
> I am writing to tell you about a "Repair Café" our university is organising next Saturday, where volunteers help people fix broken things for free.

**写法 3（提问式，亲切）**
> Have you ever thrown away a broken lamp simply because you could not fix it? Our university is going to hold a "Repair Café" to change that.

## 第二部分（邀请当志愿者）的三种写法

**写法 1（直接）**
> We are now looking for volunteers to join the team, and international students are especially welcome.

**写法 2（说你值钱）**
> Your language skills and practical experience would be a great help to us, and we would be delighted to have you on the team.

**写法 3（给好处，最像真邮件）**
> Joining us is also a good chance to practise your Chinese and meet local students. If you are interested, please contact the Student Union office before Friday.

## 完整范文（95 词）

> Dear international students,
>
> I am writing to introduce a special event called "Repair Café", which will be held in the Students' Centre next Saturday afternoon.
>
> At the event, teachers and students who are good at fixing things will help others repair broken items for free, from bicycles to small electrical appliances. Instead of throwing things away, people can give them a second life.
>
> We are now looking for volunteers to join the team, and international students are especially welcome. If you are interested, please contact the Student Union office before Friday.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Li Ming

**四段功能**：① 称呼 + 一句破题（介绍）→ ② 活动细节（做什么 + 为什么有意义）→ ③ 邀请当志愿者 + 报名方式 → ④ 落款
