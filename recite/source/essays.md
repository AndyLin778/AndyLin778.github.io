# 2021–2026 英语一写作 · 范文与思路（背诵版）

> **词数约定（刻意超过考场要求）**
> Part A 目标 **≈110 词**（考场要求约 100）｜Part B 目标 **≈220 词**（考场要求 160–200）
> **为什么多写**：背诵需要完整的信息和多余的表达——**背多写少容易，背少写多做不到**。
>
> **每题结构**：题目（原文 + 配图）→ 写作思路（审题 / 段落功能 / 内容要点）→ 范文
> **考场删减原则（12 篇通用）**：先砍**理由句、举例句、让步句**，保留**要点句和收尾句**；不要整段删，也**不能砍掉时间、地点、数字**这类内容分。宁可留 4 个短句，不要留 2 个长句。
> **题目与配图来源**：`2021-2026 英语一写作原题（含配图）.md`（图片为资料站重绘版，非官方扫描）
> **范文为我原创，非官方答案**，风格刻意避开 `First... Second... In my view... Only in this way...` 的流水线。

## 2021 · Part A ｜建议信（约 110 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
A foreign friend of yours has recently graduated from college and intends to find a
job in China. Write him/her an email to make some suggestions.
You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not sign your own name at the end. Use "Li Ming" instead.
Do not write the address. ( 10 points)
```

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **写给谁** | 外国朋友（刚毕业、想在中国找工作）→ `Dear ...` 用名字，语气亲切 |
| **我是谁** | Li Ming，中国朋友 |
| **几个要点** | ① 表示收到这个消息并愿意帮 ② **给出具体建议（2–3 条）** ③ 结尾留个后手（愿意继续帮） |
| **段落功能** | P1 开场：祝贺/高兴 + 承诺给建议 ｜ P2 建议（三条，每条要有"为什么"） ｜ P3 收尾 + 主动帮忙 |
| **最容易丢分** | 只写"要多练习中文、要努力"这种空话。**每条建议后面必须跟一句理由或具体做法** |

### 范文（110 词）

> Dear Emma,
>
> I was delighted to hear that you have decided to look for a job in China. Here are a few suggestions.
>
> First, spend the next few months on practical Chinese; most employers will forgive an accent but not a colleague who cannot follow a discussion. Second, do not restrict yourself to Beijing or Shanghai. Cities such as Hangzhou and Chengdu are full of young companies that value foreign experience and are easier to enter. Third, use your alumni network and try an internship first — many graduates here are hired by the company they interned with.
>
> Write to me if you want help with your CV.
>
> Yours,
> Li Ming

## 2021 · Part B ｜图画：喜欢就够了（约 220 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an essay of 160 - 200 words based on the following picture. In your essay, you should
1) describe the picture briefly,
2) interpret its intended meaning, and
3) give your comments.
You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
```

![2021 大作文：扮美猴王的孩子与父亲](2021-B-美猴王.jpg)

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **画面** | 左：男孩扮美猴王，手持小棒；右：父亲盘腿坐地上。气泡：孩子"很多同学觉得学唱戏不好玩"；父亲"你自己不是喜欢吗？那就足够了" |
| **寓意** | ① 兴趣 vs 同伴评价 ② **家长的支持不是"鼓励"，是"许可"**：让孩子觉得自己的喜欢是正当的 ③ 传统戏曲/非遗靠自愿才能活下去 |
| **段落功能** | P1 描述（两人 + 两句对话）｜ P2 解读（孩子怕的是"显得傻"，父亲的句子把这件事翻过来）｜ P3 引到文化传承 + 评论（家长要能分辨"一时兴起"和"真的喜欢"） |
| **避免** | 通篇写"要支持孩子""传统文化很重要"这种口号；**要点出"孩子放弃往往不是因为难，而是因为觉得自己傻"这个具体机制** |

### 范文（220 词）

> The picture shows a small boy dressed as the Monkey King, a prop stick in his hand, talking to his father, who sits cross-legged on the ground. "Dad, many of my classmates think learning opera is boring," the child says. "But you like it yourself, don't you? That is enough," the father replies.
>
> It captures a tension that every child meets: the wish to be like one's peers against the pull of something one genuinely enjoys. The boy has already learned that other people's opinions carry weight. What his father offers is not encouragement in the abstract but permission — the assurance that his own interest is a legitimate reason to go on. Children rarely give up an activity because it is difficult; they give it up because they feel foolish for caring. A parent's approval turns that feeling around.
>
> There is a broader point too. Traditional opera survives only when young people choose it freely; push them in and they leave as soon as they can. One sentence at home can do more for cultural transmission than a dozen compulsory classes.
>
> In my view, the lesson is not that parents should praise everything. It is that they should learn to tell a passing whim from a real attachment, and protect the latter even when the neighbours do not understand it.

## 2022 · Part A ｜邀请信（约 110 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an email to a professor at a British university, inviting him/her to organize
a team for the international innovation contest to be held at your university.
You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)
```

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **写给谁** | 英国某大学教授（不认识）→ `Dear Professor ...` + `Yours sincerely` |
| **我是谁** | Li Ming（学生会/学生） |
| **几个要点** | ① 邀请他组队参赛 ② 说明赛事信息（**时间、主题、规模**） ③ 附加请求（当评委/指导）+ 承诺接待 |
| **段落功能** | P1 一句点明邀请 + 赛事时间 ｜ P2 赛事细节与对他团队的期待 ｜ P3 待遇 + 请回复期限 |
| **最容易丢分** | 只写"邀请您参加"，**没有任何具体信息**，内容分很低。时间、主题、规模三样至少写两样 |

### 范文（112 词）

> Dear Professor Blake,
>
> On behalf of the Students' Union, I am writing to invite you to bring a team to the International Innovation Contest, which our university will host from 12 to 14 May.
>
> The contest brings together forty student teams from twelve countries. Each team presents a working prototype and defends it before a panel. We would be honoured if you could organise a team from your department and, if your schedule allows, serve as one of the judges on the final day.
>
> We can cover accommodation for up to five members and arrange campus transport. Please let me know before 20 March so that we can finalise the programme.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

## 2022 · Part B ｜图画：不是我们专业的（约 220 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the picture below. In your essay, you should
1) describe the picture briefly,
2) explain its intended meaning, and
3) give your comments.
You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. ( 20 points)
```

![2022 大作文：公告栏前两名女学生](2022-B-公告栏.jpg)

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **画面** | 布告栏贴"校园讲座"通知；两名女生背影（左短发、右长发）。左："不是我们专业的，听了也没用"；右："去听听肯定有好处" |
| **寓意** | ① 一个人已经把"有用"定义死了，只算能立刻用上的 ② 另一个人愿意为"暂时说不清价值"的东西花一小时 ③ **知识连接往往发生在学科交界处** ④ "有用"的判据是现在，"变成有用"由未来决定 |
| **段落功能** | P1 描述两句对话 ｜ P2 解读两种态度背后的假设 ｜ P3 说理（成本一小时 vs 可能的损失）+ 引到跨学科 ｜ P4 评论：不是懒，是把经济逻辑用错了对象 |
| **避免** | 写成"要多听讲座""要开阔视野"；**必须给出"为什么成本低、收益可能高"的推理** |

### 范文（220 词）

> Two students stand in front of a notice board where a lecture notice is posted. "It is not our major, so listening would be useless," says the one on the left. "Going to listen can only do us good," replies the other.
>
> The disagreement is small, but the assumptions behind it are not. The first student has already decided what counts as useful, and her definition is narrow: only what can be applied immediately to her coursework deserves an hour. The second is willing to spend that hour on something whose value she cannot yet name.
>
> In my view the second attitude is the more rational one. A lecture costs an hour; a missed idea may cost years. Most of the connections that later matter are formed where disciplines touch — a historian's method borrowed by a data analyst, a metaphor from biology that solves an engineering problem. Students who attend only lectures inside their own field cut themselves off from exactly those accidents.
>
> What is useful is measured by the present; what becomes useful is decided by a future we cannot see. The prudent response to that uncertainty is not to specialise earlier, but to keep a few cheap doors open.
>
> So I would not call the first student lazy. She is applying an economic logic to the wrong good.

## 2023 · Part A ｜通知：招募研究助手（约 110 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write a notice to recruit a student for Prof. Smith's research project on campus
sports activities. Specify the duties and requirements of the job.
Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name in the notice; use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)
```

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **文体** | **通知**，不是书信：居中标题 `Notice`、**无称呼**、右下署名 + 日期 |
| **要点** | ① 招募什么（Prof. Smith 的项目、招 1 名助手） ② **duties（职责）** ③ **requirements（要求）** ④ 报名方式与截止 |
| **段落功能** | 标题 ｜ P1 招募什么 ｜ P2 职责（动词并列） ｜ P3 要求 + 报名方式 ｜ 署名 + 日期 |
| **最容易丢分** | ① 加了 `Dear ...` ② 忘了日期 ③ **duties 和 requirements 混成一句，看不出谁是谁** |

### 范文（111 词）

```text
                        Notice

Prof. Smith's research project on campus sports activities is
recruiting one student assistant for the coming semester.

The assistant will collect questionnaires at the sports centre,
enter the data into a spreadsheet, and attend a short weekly
meeting with the research team. The post takes about four hours
a week and lasts until the end of December.

Applicants should be second- or third-year students who are
comfortable with basic data analysis and able to work
independently, and can write short English reports. A genuine interest in sport is welcome but not
essential.

Those who are interested should send a brief self-introduction
to the research office before 15 October.

                                        Li Ming
                                        October 8, 2026
```

## 2023 · Part B ｜图画：越来越热闹的龙舟赛（约 220 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an essay based on the picture below. In your essay, you should
1) describe the picture briefly,
2) interpret the implied meaning, and
3) give your comments.
Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
```

![2023 大作文：龙舟赛](2023-B-龙舟赛.jpg)

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **画面** | 两艘龙舟并排竞速；石拱桥上人群举臂欢呼；两岸站满观众（左岸有人举旗）；右下角老夫妇背影，老爷爷拄拐牵手。气泡："真好啊，咱们村的龙舟赛越来越热闹了" |
| **寓意** | ① 这不是"为游客演的"，是**村里自己的事** ② 传统靠"被使用"活着，不是靠"被保护" ③ 乡村叙事不该只有"空心化"一种；**能让人聚起来的事，能把年轻人拉回来几天** |
| **段落功能** | P1 描述（船、两岸、老人那句）｜ P2 指出关键：没有为游客安排 ｜ P3 重新解释"热闹"= 传统仍在使用 ｜ P4 评论：文化遗产在"仍然好玩"的地方才活 |
| **避免** | 直接跳到"要保护传统文化"。**先把"热闹"还原成"村民自己参与"**，评论才有落点 |

### 范文（224 词）

> The picture shows a dragon boat race in a riverside village. Two long boats cut through the water, paddlers straining in rhythm; crowds on both banks wave and cheer. In the foreground an old couple stand watching, the husband leaning on a stick. "How wonderful," he says, "our village's race gets livelier every year."
>
> What makes the scene worth noticing is that nothing here was arranged for tourists. The boats belong to the village, the paddlers are neighbours, and the spectators came on foot. This is a tradition alive not because it was protected but because it is still used — by people who find in it an occasion to be a community again.
>
> That matters at a time when rural life is described in terms of what it has lost: the young leaving, schools closing, streets quiet. The picture suggests another possibility. Where a custom still gives people something to do together, it can pull the young back for a few days each year, and those days are how a place remembers what it is.
>
> Seen this way, the old man's remark is not nostalgia but evidence: he is not mourning a vanishing past but reporting growth.
>
> Heritage survives where it is still enjoyed. Support that keeps a festival cheap and local is worth more than a grant that turns it into a performance.

## 2024 · Part A ｜回信：怎么准备口头报告（约 110 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Read the following email from an international student and write a reply.
---
Dear Li Ming,
I've got a class assignment to make an oral report on an ancient Chinese scientist,
but I'm not sure how to prepare for it. Can you give me some advice?
Thank you for your help.
Yours,
Paul
---
Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name in your email; use"Li Ming" instead.(10 points)
```

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **文体** | **回信**（2024–2026 连续三年考这个题型）→ 第一句必须**回应来信** |
| **要点** | ① 回应来信 ② 给建议（选人、结构、演练） ③ 主动提供后续帮助 |
| **段落功能** | P1 回应 + 表明乐意 ｜ P2 三条建议（每条带理由） ｜ P3 主动提出帮他看提纲 ｜ 落款 |
| **容易丢分** | 只写"多查资料、多练习"；建议**没有具体到可执行**（选谁、几页、练几遍） |

### 范文（113 词）

> Dear Paul,
>
> Thank you for your email. I am glad to help with your report on an ancient Chinese scientist.
>
> My first suggestion is to choose someone whose work can be shown — Zhang Heng and his seismoscope, or Li Shizhen and his herbal compendium, are far easier to present than a figure known mainly for official titles. Second, keep the structure simple: one slide on the world he lived in, one on what he actually did, one on why it still matters. Third, rehearse aloud twice; most first spoken versions run three minutes too long.
>
> If you send me your outline by Friday, I can check the historical details.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

## 2024 · Part B ｜混合题：家门口的公园（约 220 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an essay based on the picture and the chart below. In your essay, you should
1) describe the picture and the chart briefly,
2) interpret the implied meaning, and
3) give your comments.
Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
```

![2024 大作文：市民在新建公园活动 + 某市近三年公园数量](2024-B-公园与柱状图.jpg)

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **画面** | 左：公园里 5 人（2 名儿童跑动、3 名成人同行），牌子"市民健身区"，气泡"家门口新建的小公园真不错！" 右：柱状图"某市近三年公园数量（单位：座）"：406 / 532 / 670 |
| **寓意** | ① 城市治理从"建大工程"转向"身边小事" ② **关键不是绿地总量，是可步行到达的比例** ③ 风险：建得起、养不起；富裕片区维护更好 |
| **段落功能** | P1 描述两图（**picture 和 chart 都要写**）｜ P2 解读这个转向 ｜ P3 说清"家门口"的意义 ｜ P4 提风险 + 评论 |
| **最容易丢分** | Directions 是 `picture **and** the chart`，**只写漫画就是漏点** |

### 范文（218 词）

> The picture on the left shows a small new park: children running along the path, adults strolling and talking, a sign reading "citizens' fitness area", and someone remarking, "The new park at our doorstep is really nice." The bar chart on the right shows why such scenes have become common: the number of parks in one city rose from 406 in 2020 to 532 in 2021 and 670 in 2022.
>
> Behind the figures is a change in what city governments think public space is for. Parks used to be built as landmarks — large, ceremonial, and usually several bus rides away. The past few years have seen money go instead into small plots between housing blocks.
>
> That shift is worth more than its scale suggests. A park you can reach in five minutes is used every day; one thirty minutes away is visited twice a year. The word "doorstep" is the whole point: what improves daily life is not the total area of green space but the share of it within a short walk.
>
> There are risks. Small parks are cheap to build and expensive to maintain. If the coming years bring construction without maintenance, the chart will keep rising while the experience stops improving.
>
> On balance the direction is right. A city that spends on the ordinary afternoon has stopped confusing size with quality.

## 2025 · Part A ｜回信：匠人会展示什么、你能帮什么（约 110 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Read the following email from your classmate Paul and write him a reply.
---
Dear Li Ming,
I was really excited to hear that you'd invite some young craftsmen to demonstrate
their innovative craft-making on campus. May I know more about what they'll show?
Also, I'd like to help with your preparation work. Please let me know what I can do.
Yours,
Paul
---
Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name in your email; use"Li Ming" instead.(10 points)
```

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **文体** | **回信**，且**他问了两个问题**：展示什么？我能帮什么？ |
| **要点** | ① 回应来信 ② 答第一问（具体展示项，要有细节） ③ 答第二问（给他派一件具体的事） ④ 收尾 |
| **段落功能** | P1 回应 + 乐意 ｜ P2 展示什么（三项 + 每项一个细节） ｜ P3 你帮忙做什么（具体、可执行、有时间） ｜ 落款 |
| **最容易丢分** | 只答一问；或第二问只写"欢迎你来帮忙"而不给**具体任务** |

### 范文（109 词）

> Dear Paul,
>
> Thank you for your email. I am glad you are interested in the craftsmen's visit.
>
> They will demonstrate three things: bamboo weaving, in which strips are split by hand and woven into baskets without a single nail; paper-cutting, done freehand with scissors rather than a knife; and a wooden joint that lets a small figure walk. Each will give a short talk.
>
> As for your help, could you handle the English introduction for each guest? Fifteen minutes each is enough, and I will send you their notes this week. If you are free on Friday, we also need someone to test the projector.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

## 2025 · Part B ｜表格：耐用消费品拥有量（约 220 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an essay based on the table below. In your essay, you should
1) describe the table briefly,
2) interpret the table, and
3) give your comments.
Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
```

![2025 大作文：全国居民平均每百户年末主要耐用消费品拥有量](2025-B-耐用消费品表格.jpg)

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **数据** | 空调 75.2→145.9（近翻倍）｜洗衣机 83.7→98.2｜冰箱 85.5→103.4（都近饱和） |
| **寓意** | ① **对比才是重点**：老家电早饱和，空调才刚普及 ② 三个原因：规模降本、电网可靠、住房条件 ③ 饱和意味着下一阶段是**更新换代与能效**，不是增量 |
| **段落功能** | P1 描述（挑最大变化先说）｜ P2 解释对比 ｜ P3 三个原因 ｜ P4 评论：从"有没有"到"好不好" |
| **容易丢分** | 把 12 个数字全报一遍；**没有"最"**（哪个涨最快） |

### 范文（219 词）

> The table shows the average number of major durable goods owned per hundred households in China between 2014 and 2023. Air conditioners rose from 75.2 to 145.9, while washing machines and refrigerators moved far more slowly, from 83.7 to 98.2 and from 85.5 to 103.4.
>
> The contrast is the interesting part. Washing machines and refrigerators had already reached most households by 2014, so there was little room left to grow. Air conditioners, by contrast, were still a luxury in many homes a decade ago and are now close to universal, with more than one unit per household.
>
> Three forces explain the rise: manufacturing scale that pushed prices down, a grid reliable enough to carry the load, and housing that made installation possible. Behind the numbers sits a change in what people expect from an ordinary summer.
>
> The near-saturation of the older appliances carries a message for the next decade. When almost everyone already owns a washing machine, growth comes not from selling more but from selling better — quieter motors, lower energy use, longer life. The same will soon be true of air conditioners.
>
> So the table is not mainly a story about consumption. It marks the point where the question shifts from "do we have it" to "how well does it work", and where sensible policy is no longer encouragement to buy but standards for what is bought.

## 2026 · Part A ｜回信：手写信与展出（约 110 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Read the following email from your friend Paul and write him a reply.
---
Hi Li Ming,
I was really moved by the Chinese families' handwritten letters you posted
yesterday. They are priceless! Could you please tell me a bit more about them? And
are they currently on public display somewhere? I'm very keen to see them in person.
Thanks.
Yours,
Paul
```

## 2026 · Part B ｜双图表：养老机器人的接受度与担忧（约 220 词）

### 题目

```text
Directions:
Write an essay based on the charts below. In your essay, you should
1) describe the charts briefly,
2) interpret the charts, and
3) give your comments.
Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)
```

![2026 大作文：养老机器人消费者接受度与首要关注点调查](2026-B-养老机器人.jpg)

### 写作思路

| 项 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **数据** | 接受度：完全 39.3 / 部分 32.8 / 不接受 27.9 ｜ 关注点：安全 46.30 / 价格 24.90 / 便利 10.70 |
| **寓意** | ① 接受度高不是"热爱科技"，而是**需求真实**（独居老人、护理人力缺口） ② **价格只排第二**，说明阻碍不是买不起 ③ 怕的是"在最需要的那一刻失灵"，风险与用户脆弱性成正比 ④ 结论：监管、标准、责任划分比降价更关键 |
| **段落功能** | P1 描述两图（三组数字 + "安全居首"）｜ P2 解读图一（需求驱动） ｜ P3 解读图二（价格不是障碍） ｜ P4 推论 + 评论 |
| **避免** | 写成"科技要重视安全"的空话。**要给出"为什么价格不是主要障碍"这个反常识点** |

### 范文（225 词）

> The two charts report a survey on how consumers view elderly-care robots. On the left, 39.3% of the respondents fully accept such robots, 32.8% partly accept them, and 27.9% reject them. On the right, safety ranks first among their concerns at 46.30%, far above price at 24.90% and convenience at 10.70%.
>
> The first chart is easy to explain: the demand is real. With millions of elderly people living alone and nursing staff in short supply, a machine that can remind someone to take medicine or call for help is not a gadget but a substitute for a person who is not there. Acceptance here is driven by necessity rather than enthusiasm for technology.
>
> The second chart is the more revealing one. Price, the usual obstacle for a new product, comes a distant second. What people fear is a machine that fails at the moment it is trusted. That fear is proportionate: the user is frail, and the cost of error is not inconvenience but injury.
>
> If the goal is wider adoption, the decisive investment is not in cutting prices but in standards, testing and clear responsibility when something goes wrong. Reassurance, not discount, is what the market is waiting for.
>
> In my view the charts describe a society that has already decided it needs this technology, and is now asking, reasonably, whether it can be trusted with the people it loves.
